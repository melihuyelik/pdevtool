Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Overview

PDevTool is a desktop application. It runs on your computer and has no server of its own. The developer does not collect, receive, store or share any personal data.

Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect your Google account, PDevTool requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. This lets the app create, read and delete its own backup file in the hidden application data folder of your Google Drive. The app cannot see or access any other files in your Drive.

  • Your settings, notes and keys are encrypted on your device with a backup password you choose, before anything is uploaded.
  • The backup password is never uploaded or sent to the developer. Without it the backup cannot be decrypted, and a lost password cannot be recovered.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored only on your device, in the operating system's credential store.
  • Data is transferred only between your device and Google. It is not sent to the developer or any third party.

Limited Use

PDevTool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the backup and restore feature and is not used for advertising or sold.

Deleting your data

You can delete the backup from within the app, or in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps → PDevTool → Delete hidden app data. You can revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
